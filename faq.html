No, you do not need to create an account. We believe in keeping things simple. Just open the website, click on the tool you need, and start using it instantly.

Absolutely. Most of our tools (like the Image Compressor and QR Code Generator) work directly in your browser. Your files are processed on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Yes! AIFreeKit is fully responsive and optimized for all devices. You can use our tools seamlessly on your smartphone, tablet, or computer.
