What is an Image Compressor?

An image compressor is a tool that reduces the file size of an image without significantly affecting its visual quality. Large images take up a lot of storage space and make websites load slower. By using our free AIFreeKit image compressor, you can easily reduce the size of JPG, PNG, and WebP files.

Why Should You Compress Images?

1. Faster Loading: If you are a website owner, compressed images will make your website load much faster.
2. Save Storage: Keep more photos on your phone or computer without running out of space.
3. Easy Sharing: Many platforms have upload limits (like 2MB or 5MB). Compressing images helps you upload them easily to job portals, emails, and forms.

How to Use This Tool?

1. Click on the "Select an image" button above.
2. Choose the image from your phone or computer.
3. Adjust the quality slider (70% is usually best).
4. Click "Compress Image Now" and download your new file!
