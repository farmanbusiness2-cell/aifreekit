How to Compress Image Size Without Losing Quality

Published on: October 2026 | Reading time: 3 mins

In today's digital world, we often need to upload images to various platforms. Whether it's a job application form, an email attachment, or a website, there is almost always a file size limit (like 2MB or 5MB).

If your image is too large, you might face upload errors. The best solution is to compress the image. But many people worry that compressing an image will ruin its quality. The good news is, it doesn't have to!

Why Should You Compress Images?

  • Faster Website Loading: If you run a blog or website, large images slow down your site.
  • Easy Uploads: Compress images to meet the size limits of job portals and government forms.
  • Save Storage: Keep more photos on your phone without running out of space.

How to Calculate Your Exact Age (Step-by-Step)

Published on: October 2026 | Reading time: 2 mins

Knowing your exact age is important for many reasons. Whether you are applying for a government job, filling out a school admission form, or checking your retirement date, you often need your age in years, months, and days.

Why is Calculating Exact Age Tricky?

Most people just subtract their birth year from the current year. But that doesn't give the exact age. You have to consider leap years, the number of days in each month, and your exact birth date. Doing this manually can lead to mistakes.

How to Use Our Free Age Calculator

You don't need to do complex math. Our free tool does it for you in seconds:

  1. Go to the AIFreeKit Home Page.
  2. Click on the 🎂 Age Calculator tool.
  3. Select your Date of Birth from the calendar.
  4. Click "Calculate Age".
  5. Instantly see your exact age in Years, Months, and Days, along with your total days lived!
