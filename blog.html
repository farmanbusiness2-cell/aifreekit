What is EMI and How to Calculate It? (Simple Guide)

Published on: October 2026 | Reading time: 3 mins

If you are planning to buy a house, a car, or need an education loan, you have definitely heard the term EMI. But what exactly does it mean, and how does it affect your monthly budget?

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. When you take a loan, the bank gives you the full amount, and you pay it back in small parts (EMIs) over a few years.

Why is Calculating EMI Important?

Before taking any loan, it is very important to know your EMI. If your EMI is too high, it can ruin your monthly budget. Calculating EMI in advance helps you decide how much loan you can afford.

How to Use Our Free EMI Calculator

Have you noticed those small square barcodes everywhere? On restaurant menus, product packaging, and shop windows? These are called QR (Quick Response) codes. They are the fastest way to share information today.

What is a QR Code?

A QR code is a type of barcode that can be read easily by a digital device like a smartphone camera. It stores information such as a website link, text, phone number, or WiFi password. When you scan it, the information opens instantly on your phone.

Why Should You Use a QR Code?

Why You Need a Strong Password (And How to Create One)

Published on: October 2026 | Reading time: 3 mins

In today's digital world, our entire lives are on the internet. From social media accounts to bank details, everything is protected by a single thing: Your Password.

Most people use easy-to-remember passwords like their name, date of birth, or "123456". But did you know that hackers can crack these weak passwords in just a few seconds using automated software?

Why Weak Passwords are Dangerous

If a hacker gets access to your email or social media, they can steal your personal information, send spam to your contacts, or even access your bank accounts. Using the same password for multiple websites is even more dangerous, because if one website gets hacked, all your accounts become vulnerable.

What Makes a Password Strong?

A strong password should have these three things:

  • Length: It should be at least 12-16 characters long.
  • Complexity: It should include a mix of Uppercase letters (A-Z), Lowercase letters (a-z), Numbers (0-9), and Special Symbols (!@#$%).
  • Unpredictability: It should not contain any dictionary words, names, or common patterns.
