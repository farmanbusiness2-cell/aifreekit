If you are planning to buy a house, a car, or need an education loan, you have definitely heard the term EMI. But what exactly does it mean, and how does it affect your monthly budget?

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. When you take a loan, the bank gives you the full amount, and you pay it back in small parts (EMIs) over a few years.

Why is Calculating EMI Important?

Before taking any loan, it is very important to know your EMI. If your EMI is too high, it can ruin your monthly budget. Calculating EMI in advance helps you decide how much loan you can afford.
